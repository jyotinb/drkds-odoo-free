Material Request (Lite)

Let employees request materials from stock and approve them, on Odoo 19 Community

Odoo 19 Community moves goods between locations perfectly well. What it has no document for is the step before that: an employee asking for the material, and somebody deciding whether they get it. Purchase Agreements covers vendor tenders and blanket orders, which is a different question entirely. This module adds the missing internal document, and nothing else.

The Material Requests list filtered on My Requests: five requests with reference, requester, department, request and required dates, an outstanding quantity per request totalling 197.00, and Draft, Submitted and Approved statuses.

What it does

A numbered request

Sequence reference, requester, department, request date, required-by date, warehouse, source and destination location, reason and status — on one legible document.

Three quantities, kept apart

Every line tracks what was asked for, what has actually been delivered and what is still outstanding. The delivered figure is read back from the stock moves, never typed in.

On hand where it matters

Each line shows the real quantity available at the request's own source location, converted into the line's unit of measure, so the approver decides on facts rather than a guess.

Submit, approve, refuse

One approver group and one decision. A refusal must carry a reason. The state machine refuses every transition it does not define, and nobody can approve a request they raised themselves.

Fulfilment is a normal transfer

Approving creates an internal transfer for the outstanding lines and links each move back to its line. Validate it and the request updates itself — the stock side stays entirely standard.

Closes itself

A partial delivery leaves the request open, and a further transfer can be raised for the remainder. Once every line is delivered in full the request moves to Done on its own.

Good to know

  • Depends on Inventory and Employees only, both Odoo 19 Community. Nothing from Enterprise.
  • Two groups: a Requester sees only their own requests, an Approver sees them all.
  • List, Kanban by status, form and search views, with My Requests, Awaiting My Approval and Outstanding filters.
  • The approval step is one step and stays one step. There is no configurable approval engine here.
  • No outbound calls of any kind. Everything stays in your database.
