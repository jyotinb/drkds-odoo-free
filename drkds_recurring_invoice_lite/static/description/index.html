Recurring Invoices (Lite)

Issue the same customer invoice on a schedule: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, on Odoo 19 Community

Rent, retainers, maintenance contracts, subscriptions: the same invoice goes to the same customer at the same interval. Describe it once as a template, say how often it repeats and start it. A daily scheduler issues a real customer invoice on every due date, dated on that date, and links it back to the template. Pause it, resume it, close it on an end date, or press Generate Now when the customer wants it early.

What it does

One template per contract

Customer, journal, currency, payment terms, terms and conditions, and the invoice lines with product, description, quantity, unit price and taxes. Totals are shown on the template so you know what will go out.

Any interval

Every N days, weeks, months or years, from a start date, with an optional end date after which the template marks itself done.

A scheduler that never doubles up

One invoice per template and date, however many times the job runs or the button is pressed. A scheduler that was off for a while catches up with one invoice per missed date.

Month-end dates that hold

A monthly template set on the 31st invoices on 28 February and is back on the 31st in March. A yearly one set on 29 February falls to the 28th in common years and returns on the next leap year.

Draft or posted

Leave each generated invoice in draft for a glance before it goes out, or let the template post it straight away.

A trail both ways

The template shows its invoices on a smart button with the count and the last invoice date; every invoice names the template it came from in Other Info and in the invoice search.

Good to know

  • Depends on Accounting (account) only. The generated documents are ordinary customer invoices numbered by the sale journal.
  • Access follows Accounting: billing users create and run templates, read-only accounting users can look at them. Multi-company record rules are in place.
  • A template that has issued invoices cannot be deleted, only archived, so the trail on its invoices stays intact.
  • If one template cannot be invoiced (say a posting is refused), the scheduler records the reason on that template, leaves it to be retried tomorrow, and carries on with the others.
  • Odoo's own Auto-post recurrence duplicates a posted entry monthly, quarterly or yearly. This module works from an editable template instead, adds daily and weekly intervals, pause and resume, an end date and a Generate Now button.
