Knowledge Base (Lite)

Internal articles in a section tree, with rich text, tags and search, on Odoo 19 Community

A place to write down how your company actually does things, and a way to find it again six months later. Articles are rich text, filed in a tree of sections, tagged, searched and bookmarked - all inside Odoo, with nothing sent anywhere else.

What it does

Sections that nest

Build a tree as deep as the subject deserves. Every section carries its full path, so 'First Week' is never ambiguous - it reads as Handbook / Onboarding / First Week.

Articles that read like pages

Rich text with headings, lists, tables and images, on a comfortable measure. Title, section, tags, author, last editor and status sit quietly above the text instead of crowding it.

Search that reaches the body

The search box looks inside the article, not only at the title. A word that appears once in the third paragraph is still findable, because every save keeps a flattened plain text copy alongside the formatted one.

A sidebar tree while you browse

Sections appear beside the article list with live counts. Pick a branch and everything below it shows, including articles filed in sub-sections.

Filters, group-bys and what changed

Filter by section, tag, author, status or recency. Group by section, author, last editor, status or month. A Recently Updated view answers 'what changed while I was away'.

Private bookmarks

One click keeps an article in My Bookmarks. Bookmarks are per user and stored separately from the article, so someone with read-only access can still keep their own list.

Need the full version? The drkds Wiki Articles app adds article versioning with rollback, portal sharing and approval workflows.

Good to know

  • Two groups: Reader sees published articles and keeps bookmarks; Editor writes articles, manages sections and tags, and is the only one who sees drafts.
  • Access is enforced by record rules, not by hiding buttons - a reader cannot reach a draft by any route.
  • The last editor is stamped automatically whenever the text, the filing or the status changes, so it is never stale.
  • Section nesting uses a materialised path, so 'everything under this branch' stays one indexed query as the tree grows.
  • Full chatter on every article: comments, followers and activities. Depends on the mail module only.
