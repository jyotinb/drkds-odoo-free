# Changelog

## 19.0.1.0.0 - 2026-09-15

First release.

- Ticket model with sequence numbering, customer, assignee, team, stage,
  priority and tags, on the mail thread and activity mixins.
- Support teams with members and a default assignee applied on create.
- Ordered stages, global or team specific, with folded and closing flags.
- Closing date stamped on entering a closing stage and cleared on reopening.
- Kanban, list, form and search views, plus a pivot and bar chart analysis.
- Support user and support manager groups with record rules.
